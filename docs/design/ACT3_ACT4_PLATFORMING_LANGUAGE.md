# Act 3–4 platforming language rebuild

This companion to `ACT1_ACT2_PLATFORMING_LANGUAGE.md` records the production language for Neon Moon Protocol and Abyssal Night. It describes the collision-visible traversal contract rather than replacing gameplay blueprints or the generated-art provenance record.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Lunar routes read as orbital infrastructure: crater bridges, pressure locks, cleanroom spines, security shafts, and command-ring platforms have supports, rails, emitters, or containment housings.
2. Abyssal routes read as collapsing bio-industrial mass: void bridges have buttresses, organic ribs, cable anchors, veins, and illuminated corruption nodes rather than generic city catwalks.
3. Low/inverted gravity, terminal control, laser security, corruption, and void hazards expose their source at the traversal beat. Palette alone never communicates a mechanic.
4. Vertical travel needs background overscan and a continuous structural landmark. The Security Grid Shaft is a shaft and lift system, not a stack of floating decks.
5. Final-act mastery routes can combine mechanics, but their deck silhouette and foreground cue must still tell the player whether a surface is stable, moving, dangerous, or optional.

## Act 3 — Neon Moon Protocol

| Stage | Route beat | Structural language |
|---|---|---|
| 3-1 Lunar Surface Arrival | Low-gravity landing and long gap | Crater-span rails, cyan emitter pylons, and lunar perimeter supports. |
| 3-2 Research Cleanrooms | Controlled security puzzle | Pressure-lock airlocks, observation spines, glass containment, and cleanroom bridge frames. |
| 3-3 Security Grid Shaft | Vertical laser/turret ascent | Continuous security shaft, orbital lift, guided rails, and vertically overscanned background field. |
| 3-4 Bio-Tech Labs | Gravity reversal and containment bypass | Breached containment bridges, bio-tech supports, and glowing containment reservoirs. |
| 3-5 Orbital Command | Oracle arena | Symmetric command-ring assembly and a protected orbital telemetry core. |

## Act 4 — Abyssal Night

| Stage | Route beat | Structural language |
|---|---|---|
| 4-1 Corrupted Outpost | Corruption escalation and lift | Broken purifier stairs, corruption-lit support legs, and a failing outpost lift. |
| 4-2 The Dark Chasm | Void timing and cable traversal | Cable lifts, shadow bridge anchors, and deep chasm buttresses. |
| 4-3 Bio-Mechanical Nest | Organic-crusher route | Rib walks, brood bypasses, nodal veins, and nest-scale containment tissue. |
| 4-4 Abyssal Sanctuary | Multi-mechanic mastery | Sanctuary steps, void carriage, reliquary supports, and framed sanctuary thresholds. |
| 4-5 Heart of the Void | Void Cerberus finale | Stable radial arena supports surrounding the visible void heart; combat danger never hides the arena edge. |
